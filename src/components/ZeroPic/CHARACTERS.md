# What Zero, Ciel and Lumine look like

Canonical character references for any agent drawing, describing or prompting
images of Twinkle's three AI characters. These files sit here even when the
website doesn't import them: the pictures are the source of truth.

## Art style (all three)
Anime cel-shading: bold clean black ink outlines, glossy highlights on hair and
armor, big expressive blue eyes, saturated flat color with soft shading, and
painterly backgrounds with bokeh light dots. Never Dragon Ball / Toriyama style,
and never copy designs from an existing franchise.

## Zero
A boy android, the cool "brother" of the pair. Spiky cyan/teal hair and blue
eyes. He wears a white collared shirt with white sleeves, a blue vest (no long
jacket), a red tie, and rounded light blue-gray armored shoulder pauldrons. He
is calm and cool but not stern: in the group picture he winks with a small,
confident half-smile.

## Ciel
A girl android, the "sister". Short teal bob, blue eyes, and a white rounded
helmet with glowing red-and-cyan ear pieces. She wears a glossy white armor
bodysuit with one large orange oval gem centered on the chest, a navy high
collar, and big rounded white shoulder armor. Her color is magenta/pink.
Composed and quietly confident.

## Lumine
Twinkle's build platform, drawn as a big friendly guardian robot, never a
humanoid. It has a rounded navy head with a wide glowing cyan visor (two oval
eyes) in a gold rim, round cyan side lenses and a gold cap on top. Its chest is
a gold-barred glass cage holding the big glowing golden four-point star (the
Lumine logo, `twinkle-vite/public/lumine-icon-512.png`), with floating sparks
inside. It has huge round navy shoulders with gold bands, white blocky fists,
and short navy legs on rounded gold-plated feet. The armor is deep navy (about
#1c2541) with warm gold trim. It is cute and gentle, and in group pictures it
stands behind Zero and Ciel as their protector, never menacing.
